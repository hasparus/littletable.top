# Rider–Waite–Smith tarot artwork

Pamela Colman Smith illustrated these 78 cards for Arthur Edward Waite. William Rider & Son published the Roses & Lilies printing in 1909. Saskia Jansen scanned the cards from her personal collection. Little Table uses those scans at 640 pixels high, re-encoded as WebP.

The 1909 artwork is in the public domain in the United States and in jurisdictions with terms of life plus 70 years or less. Wikimedia Commons applies [Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) to the individual scan files as well. The file pages identify the images as scans of the original cards and describe no separate copyright claim on the reproductions.

[Wikimedia Commons collection](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)>)

## Card back

The stack uses the original 1909 Roses & Lilies back scanned by Saskia Jansen. Wikimedia Commons marks the design public domain in the United States and the United Kingdom and lists the original artist as unknown, possibly Pamela Colman Smith. The Commons copy was rotated and cropped. Little Table resized it to 640 pixels high and re-encoded it as WebP. [Card back scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waite%E2%80%93Smith_Tarot_Roses_and_Lilies_cropped.jpg)
