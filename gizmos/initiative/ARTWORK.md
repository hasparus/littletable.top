# Initiative rail portraits

The portraits crop Pamela Colman Smith’s court cards from the 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith Roses & Lilies tarot. Saskia Jansen scanned the cards from her collection. The source scans carry [Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/). The original illustrations are public domain in the United States and jurisdictions with terms of life plus 70 years or less.

Little Table crops and resizes the face and upper costume, then encodes each portrait as WebP. The preview reuses two of these crops. The [tarot package’s artwork notes](../tarot/ARTWORK.md) document the source scans in full.
