Features

  • Join from a link with just a name. Sign in to make boards and find them again.
  • Throw dice across the board with a mouse or finger, or type /r 3d6.
  • Chat with the table. Point at things with the laser.
  • Follow someone from People. The owner and managers can summon a person to their view.
  • Add a shape to Places to jump back to it.
  • Search finds text on the board and inside images, such as maps and character sheets.
  • On your own board, draw on the secret layer. Only you see it. Paste shapes onto the shared board to reveal them.
  • Share the board link or export a page as PNG. Owners can publish the PNG at a read-only link.
  • Download the board as a .tldr file. Import one to add its pages.
  • Make a player a manager in My boards. Managers can connect an AI assistant over MCP.

Dice notation

In board chat, type /r 2d20kl1 or /roll 2d20kl1. You can also enter the expression in the dice panel.

Roll20 rules apply by default. Prefix an expression with foundry:, avrae:, rpg:, maiden:, or rolisteam: to use a different meaning shown below.

Expressions can start with up to 20 dice and make up to 100 draws, including extra dice and rerolls. Standard dice can have up to 1,000,000 sides. Expressions are limited to 256 characters.

Prefixes cover the differences listed here. Character-sheet references, macros, and private-roll commands are outside the expression grammar.
